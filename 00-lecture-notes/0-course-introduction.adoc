== Introduction

This document contains information about the _Desenvolvimento de Aplicações Web_ course, for the 2026/2027 Fall Semester.

== Grading

Student evaluation and grading is composed of two components:

* Final individual written exam, done on the regular exam dates. 
    ** 30% of the final course grade.
    ** A minimum grade of 9.5 is required.

* Exercise sets and final project, done during the first 15 weeks.
    ** 70% of the final course grade.
    ** Divided into three _parts_: two exercise sets and a final project.
    ** Each part will have a discussion and a per-student grade.
    ** A minimum grade of 8.0 is required for each part.
    ** A minimum grade of 9.5 is required for this practical component.
    ** Done by groups of three students.

== Syllabus and calendar

The syllabus and associated calendar are available at link:../calendar.md[Calendar]. This calendar also has some of the important dates, namely:

* When the exercise sets and final project requirements are _published_.
* When the solutions must be _delivered_ by the student groups.
* When the discussions are done.

The course is divided into three parts:

* First-part - development of _frontends_ for browser-based applications.
* Second-part - development of _backends_ for Web-based systems.
* Third-part - complementary subjects and the development of the final project.

There will be a summary published for each lecture containing:

* The subjects of that class.
* Additional study resources.

== Resources
